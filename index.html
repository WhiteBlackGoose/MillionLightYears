Flying

Right mouse
Change course toward the cursor. Hold to keep following it. The ship first drifts on the old way; above cruise speed every turn costs speed, and 90° or more means a full stop. Arrow keys work too
W S
Throttle up and down
A D
Roll
Shift
Boost. Keep holding to go relativistic. You slow by yourself near any surface
X
Brake hard
Left drag
Look around without changing course
Double-click
Set what the cursor is on as waypoint. It stays, and an arrow points to it when it is out of view
T
Target what the cursor is on, press again to cycle
F
Glide to the target on autopilot
0 to 9
0 full stop, 1 cruise, 2 to 9 gears. Gears 2 and 3 run from 10 to 1000 km/s, 4 from 0.005 c to 0.9 c, and from 5 on every subgear adds a nine, up to 9.9 = c − 10⁻⁵¹ c. Same gear again: automatic. They also set the autopilot
[ ] or wheel
One subgear down or up, through all gears
Esc
Pause
G
Land: at the site you double-clicked on the ground, or right below. Again: take off
E
Leave the seat and walk through the ship, any time. In the blue tube Space goes up, X down. Use seat, star map and airlock with E. Outside: WASD, Shift to run, Space for jetpack
L
Leave the planet: takes off and climbs to 0.7 of its diameter
Y
Escape a gravity field too strong to hold: turns away from it and burns out
V
Relativistic view: what you would see, or what you would measure (pure length contraction)
C
Gaze: orbit the target, hands off
M
Map: zoom from the universe down to every single star
I
Data sheet of what the cursor is on, otherwise of the target
H
Hide the interface
N
Sound on / off
